{% trans "Why it exists" %}

{% trans "A shared workspace for target prioritization" %}

{% trans "From genome-scale evidence to focused biological review." %}

{% trans "The platform brings together genome annotation, off-target analysis, essentiality, structural models, pocket predictions, ligand evidence, drug-likeness descriptors, and curated project data. The goal is to help biology and bioinformatics teams move from thousands of proteins to a smaller set of target candidates that can be discussed, filtered, ranked, and inspected with their evidence trail visible." %}
